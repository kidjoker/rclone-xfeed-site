Privacy Policy

Application: rclone-xfeed · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy describes how rclone-xfeed, a personal-use OAuth configuration for the rclone tool, accesses and handles Google user data.

1. Information accessed

With the account holder's explicit permission, rclone may access Google Drive files and folders, associated file metadata, and the information necessary to complete Google OAuth authorization. The specific access granted depends on the scopes shown in Google's consent screen.

2. How information is used

Access is used solely to perform file operations requested and configured by the account holder, including browsing, uploading, downloading, copying, and synchronizing files. The application is not intended to use Google Drive data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and rclone configuration data may be stored on devices or systems configured by the account holder. Files may be copied or synchronized to destinations chosen by the account holder, including local storage or other remote storage services. This site itself does not receive, host, or collect Google Drive files, OAuth tokens, or account data.

Google user data is not sold to advertisers. Any transfer to another storage destination occurs only when configured by the account holder; the privacy and security practices of that destination may also apply.

4. Security

Google account authorization is handled through Google's OAuth process. The account holder is responsible for securing the devices, systems, configuration files, tokens, and destinations used with rclone.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens and transferred files remain until removed by the account holder or according to the retention rules of the selected destination. Access can be revoked from the Google Account connections page. Revoking access does not automatically delete files previously copied to another location; those copies must be deleted separately.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the way rclone-xfeed is used changes. The latest version will be published at this URL.
